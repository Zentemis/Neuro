FAQ

Questions you should ask

Skepticism is healthy. Here are the answers.

How do I know the AI actually trades?

Every trade is on-chain and verifiable. The bot's wallet address is public — you can trace every swap on Solscan. The dashboard pulls live data from on-chain events, not self-reported numbers.

What happens if the bot loses money?

Losses are bounded. The bot uses strict per-trade risk limits (max 2% of treasury per position). Losing trades happen — the edge is in the aggregate over hundreds of trades. No single trade can drain the treasury.

How are staking rewards calculated?

50% of trading profits are distributed proportionally to stakers based on their share of total staked $NRF. Distributions happen every cycle (approximately weekly). The more you stake, the larger your share.

Is this a rug?

Fair question. Here's what we've done to earn trust: the staking contract is immutable once deployed, the trading wallet is public, team tokens vest over 12 months, and the code is open-source. We can't rug what we can't access. But always DYOR — never invest more than you can afford to lose.

Why pump.fun instead of a regular DEX?

pump.fun gives us instant liquidity and fair launch mechanics — no presale, no VC allocation. Everyone buys at the same price. It's the most transparent launch mechanism on Solana right now.
